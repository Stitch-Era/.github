## What Is Stitch Era

Stitch Era is a local Windows embroidery editor for designs you already keep. It gives you a named stitch desk, a short hoop list, and a design view that points back to the same file. It does not try to own every window on the PC. It sits beside the stitches you already use and holds only the hoop that is open now.
The idea behind Stitch Era own designs is small on purpose: one desk, one list, one path back. Extra hoops wait for their own title. Stitch Era starts with a local profile and does not need a complex account system.

## Starting with Stitch Era

Prerequisites: A PC you own, designs you already keep, and permission to open a local helper.

1. **Prepare:** Close leftover untitled hoops and open the last Stitch Era session on a design you keep.
2. **Configure:** Read the stitch desk and the hoop list, then drop anything that belongs to a different job.
3. **Test:** Finish one visible design view inside Stitch Era own designs.
4. **Refine:** Write what changed, then close with a shorter hoop list than you found.
5. **Apply:** Keep Stitch Era stitch desk on the same file during the next session.
6. **Maintain:** If the desk feels crowded, leave extra hoops off the live set.
